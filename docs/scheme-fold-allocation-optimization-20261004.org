#+title: Scheme fold list-allocation optimization — 2026-10-04

* Scope and evidence boundary
The Scheme-owned fold is optimized directly; Rust continues to project its native
event tape. Neither runtime ownership feature, concurrency, Gambit kernel, nor
workspace dev/release profiles was changed. No remote mutation was performed.

Three incremental experiments were isolated from the frozen compiled baseline
=target/scheme-receipt-libs/inner-state-baseline-20261004=:
1. Complete the line walk with the closing-event tail, eliminating full-list EOF copies.
2. Cache only join-once immutable IR-shape admission, request-local and capped at 128 entries.
   Runtime state and lexical-index shadowing checks remain live even on a cache hit.
3. Share the final statement's forward event tail and reverse only its preceding prefix.

The first two alone do not establish a consistent latency gain. The final combined
candidate has deterministic allocation reductions in all 120 candidate samples:

historical numbers below are not current ASP qualification evidence; rerun using
